\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.62\textwidth}
\small
This report documents an investigation prompted by a legitimate
methodological concern: the internal test scores of the production
shelf-life models were unusually high, and the evaluation protocol that
produced them had not been examined at the level of the synthetic
data-generation structure.

\smallskip
The investigation reproduces the original training and evaluation
procedure from source, executes the diagnostic experiments that were
requested, identifies the specific property of the evaluation design
responsible for the optimistic scores, and re-evaluates seventeen model
configurations under a corrected protocol.

\smallskip
Every number reported here is generated from result files in the project
repository. Where the repository does not contain the evidence needed to
answer a question, that is stated explicitly rather than filled in.
\end{minipage}
\hfill
\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.32\textwidth}
\footnotesize\color{softgrey}
\textbf{\color{burgundy}Scope}\\
Six production specialists\\
Four production model families\\
Seventeen evaluated configurations\\
Six diagnostic experiments\\
Two evaluation protocols

\medskip
\textbf{\color{burgundy}Dataset}\\
V7 specialist corpus\\
34\,000 synthetic rows\\
7--12 generation rules per specialist

\medskip
\textbf{\color{burgundy}Prepared for}\\
Two-hour technical review
\end{minipage}
